FantasyWizard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

FantasyWizard (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates a fantasy basketball companion app that works with your Yahoo Fantasy Sports and ESPN Fantasy Basketball leagues. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (“App”) and related backend services.

FantasyWizard is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Yahoo or ESPN. Yahoo and ESPN are trademarks of their respective owners.

1. How Sign-In Works

To show your leagues, you sign in to Yahoo or ESPN on that provider’s own login page, which opens inside the App. We never see or store your password. After you sign in, the App reads the login cookies that the provider’s website sets in that in-app browser:

  • Yahoo: the T and Y cookies
  • ESPN: the espn_s2 and SWID cookies

These cookies work like a temporary login session for your account, which is why we handle them with care (see sections 2, 5 and 6). The in-app browser is cleared before each sign-in, so you can sign in with a different account. The sign-in pages belong to Yahoo and ESPN; their privacy policies apply to what they collect, including if you choose to sign in through a third-party service such as Google or Apple on their page.

Our access is read-only. We cannot change, post to, or delete anything in your Yahoo or ESPN account.

2. Information We Collect

  • Login cookies: stored in your device’s secure storage. The App sends them to our backend over HTTPS with each request, so the backend can read your league data from Yahoo or ESPN on your behalf. They are not saved in our database or written to our logs, and they may stay in the server’s memory only briefly while requests are processed.
  • League data from Yahoo and ESPN: your leagues, teams, rosters, matchups, and standings, retrieved with your login. ESPN does not list a user’s leagues, so the league IDs you add, and the names of those leagues, are saved on your device.
  • Account identifier: to keep cached data consistent, our backend uses a one-way hash of your Yahoo or ESPN account identifier as an internal key. For Yahoo, we also keep your Yahoo account identifier for up to 30 days, keyed by a hash of your login, so your cached data is found again after your cookies refresh.
  • Cached data: we temporarily cache your league data on our servers to improve speed and reduce load. Cached data expires automatically (see section 6).
  • Anonymous app ID, usage, and crash data: the App creates a random anonymous ID on your device and uses it with Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to fix crashes. This includes which features are used, screen views, device and app version, and crash logs. The ID is not linked to your Yahoo or ESPN account. We do not use Firebase for advertising, marketing, or cross-app tracking.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the App signs your device up for game reminders through Firebase Cloud Messaging. We do not store your device token on our servers.
  • Server error reports: when our backend hits an error, technical details are sent to Sentry. Login information is removed from these reports before they are sent.
  • App updates and integrity: the App checks for updates through Shorebird and uses Firebase App Check to confirm it is a genuine copy of the App. These checks send technical information such as app version and platform.

The App’s demo mode shows sample data stored inside the App. It needs no account and sends no login information.

3. How We Use Information

  • Show your fantasy basketball data in the App
  • Provide analysis, projections, and suggestions for your fantasy teams
  • Send game reminders you have allowed
  • Keep the App reliable, secure, and fast

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who We Share Data With

We share data only with the services needed to run the App:

  • Yahoo and ESPN – your login is sent to them to retrieve your league data
  • Google Firebase – analytics, crash reports, notifications, and app integrity checks
  • Sentry – server error reports, with login information removed
  • Shorebird – delivery of app updates
  • Railway – hosting for our backend and its temporary cache

We do not share your data with anyone else, except if required by law.

5. Data Security

All communication between the App, our backend, and Yahoo or ESPN uses HTTPS. Login cookies are kept in your device’s secure storage, and our server tools are set up to keep them out of logs and error reports. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Data Retention

  • On your device: your login cookies, saved ESPN leagues, and settings stay until you disconnect or delete the App.
  • On our servers: cached league data expires automatically within 30 minutes to 24 hours, depending on the type of data. The Yahoo account identifier described above is kept for up to 30 days. General NBA schedule and player statistics, which are not personal data, may be cached for up to a week.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase according to its retention settings.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Disconnect: in the App, open the menu, then Connections. You can disconnect Yahoo or ESPN, or choose “Disconnect everything”, to remove your login and saved leagues from your device.
  • End access completely: signing out of Yahoo or ESPN on their website, or changing your password, invalidates the cookies the App holds.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Requests: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To ask about your data or request deletion of cached data, email alperenekin97@gmail.com.

8. Children’s Privacy

FantasyWizard is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact
